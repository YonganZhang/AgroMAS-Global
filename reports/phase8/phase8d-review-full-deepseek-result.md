## Findings

### F1 — Moderate: P2 predictive results are pooled across Former-Held-Out and Public-Reused, contradicting the "cohorts unpooled" claim
**References.** `manuscript/phase8/en.md:41` and `zh.md:41` state: "NASA and WUR sources, Former-Held-Out and Public-Reused cohorts, and LOYO and LORO results remain unpooled." The abstract (`en.md:9`) says "sources, cohorts and transfer schemes kept separate." `reports/phase8/PHASE8D_TOP_JOURNAL_REPORT.md:23` repeats the unpooled claim. The P2 predictive artifact `reports/phase8/phase8d-P2-adapter-training-freeze.json` reports only scheme/variant aggregates; `scripts/phase8d-fit-p2-adapters.py:47` computes `Grain_N_MSE_pooled`, `Grain_N_MSE_world_equal`, `prior_acceptance_rows` and `prior_acceptance_world_equal` over all 44 P2 site-years, and `:22-26` loops over `sorted(set(world))` including the 4 `Public-Reused` worlds.

**Evidence.** The 44 P2 site-years are 40 Former-Held-Out + 4 Public-Reused (NL 2018–2021); `cache/phase8d-p2-adapter-inputs/metadata.joblib` `cohort` confirms 40/4. Computed from `models/phase8d-controls/p2-adapters/*-OOF.npz` and the metadata:
- LOYO Full64 / Static3: all-44 row-pooled 462.87 / 465.41; Former-only 452.02 / 454.04; Public-only 549.91 / 556.60. All-44 world-equal 455.29 / 452.59; Former-only 445.83 / 442.19.
- Prior acceptance rows, all-44: LOYO 89.65%, LORO 75.12%; Former-only 88.36%, 72.02%; Public-only 100%, 100%. World-equal, all-44: LOYO 79.55%, LORO 63.64%; Former-only 77.50%, 60.00%; Public-only 100%, 100%. LORO Full64 / Static3 MSE: all-44 505.05 / 492.41; Former-only 494.84 / 481.10; Public-only 586.98 / 583.26.

**Impact.** The reported 462.87/455.29/89.65%, 505.05/492.41 and 75.12%/63.64% are not Former-held-out values; they include four Public-Reused NL site-years with 100% acceptance and higher MSE. A reader can infer Former n=40 from the preceding sentence in `en.md:53`, but the numbers are all-44. The qualitative conclusion is unchanged (row-pooled LOYO slightly favors Full64; world-equal LOYO and LORO do not; LORO MSE worse), but the "cohorts unpooled" sentence and the exact P2 numbers are inconsistent. This is a reporting/stratification defect, not a reason to retune.

**Reproduction.** Load the P2 metadata and OOF arrays and split by `cohort`; e.g. `python3` with `joblib.load('cache/phase8d-p2-adapter-inputs/metadata.joblib')`, `numpy.load('cache/phase8d-p2-adapter-inputs/y.npy')`, `numpy.load('models/phase8d-controls/p2-adapters/LOYO-Full64-OOF.npz')` reproduces the values above. No source change is needed to reproduce.

**Suggested correction.** Either add cohort-stratified P2 results to the artifact and prose (Former 40 / Public 4), or label the diagnostic "pooled over Former-Held-Out + Public-Reused" and soften the unpooled sentence/abstract. Do not retune or re-fit.

### F2 — Low-Moderate: exact sign-test p-values are pooled farm-year while the reported effects/CIs are equal-region
**References.** `scripts/phase8d-analyze-results.py:51` overrides `mean_difference` and `CI95` with equal-region values but leaves the exact test from `lib.paired(...)`, tagging `exact_test_weighting='pooled paired farm-year signs/ranks,not region-weighted ranks'`. `manuscript/phase8/en.md:41` only says "Descriptive intervals resample paired years within region for equal-region contrasts. Every reported exact two-sided sign test belongs to one Holm family."

**Evidence.** `all-contrasts.json` GLOBAL_EQUAL5 (TreeMLP Sensorless soft vs SensorlessPPO Sensorless tree, joint): `mean_difference=-0.01939393939393939`, `CI95=[-0.08545454545454546,0.04090909090909091]`, `exact_two_sided_sign_p=1.0`, `Holm_sign_p_full_family=1.0`, `n=52`, `n_eff_nonzero=6`, `ties=46`, `exact_test_weighting="pooled paired farm-year signs/ranks,not region-weighted ranks"`. The prose reports −1.94 pp, −8.55 to +4.09, p=1, without noting the p is pooled.

**Impact.** The effect and interval are equal-region, the p is pooled over 52 farm-years. For this contrast p=1 under either weighting, so no inferential conclusion changes; but the Methods sentence can be read as a single weighting, and the 13,512-contrast family mixes equal-region effects with pooled p-values. This is a transparency/strata issue, not a numerical contradiction in the headline.

**Suggested correction.** Add "pooled paired farm-year sign test" to the p-value description (or compute an equal-region sign test where feasible). No retuning.

### F3 — Low: P2 closed-loop and supplement "Joint(%)" are equal-region but not labelled
**References.** `manuscript/phase8/en.md:53` reports P2 closed-loop 29.83/27.61/20.39/17.89 but does not say "equal-region". These are `region_equal` values; pooled values are 30.00/27.50/20.00/17.50 (`phase8d-core-statistics.json` summary rows). `scripts/phase8d-build-supplement.py:11` and `manuscript/phase8/supplement.tex:8` use header `Joint(\%)` while the cells are `100*x['region_equal']['joint']`. `manuscript/phase8/README.md` says "All exported metrics are equal-region means", but the supplement PDF does not.

**Impact.** A reader may interpret P2/supplement values as pooled. The differences are small but real (e.g., 25.06 vs 25.00; 29.83 vs 30.00; 20.39 vs 20.00), and the P1 text is explicit while P2/supplement are not.

**Suggested correction.** Label the P2 sentence and supplement header "equal-region" or add both equal-region and pooled columns.

### F4 — Low: Figure 3 omits Public-Reused LORO and its panel c is Former-only/unlabelled
**References.** `scripts/phase8d-final-figures.py:34` plots only `Former LOYO`, `Public NL LOYO` and `Former LORO`; there is no Public LORO group, although `en.md:53` reports Public-Reused LORO 25.00/25.00. `:13` defines `get(..., cohort='Former-Held-Out')` and `:40-43` panel c calls `get(p, benchmark='P2', scheme=scheme)` without a cohort, so it is Former-only; its y-axis "Accepted prior decisions (%)" is the closed-loop Former metric (e.g., P2-Full64 LORO Former `prior_accepted=1.8`, `prior_decisions=3.0` → 60.0%), while the text's 75.12% is the predictive-diagnostic all-44 row acceptance. The caption (`phase8d-captions.json`, `en.tex`) says "P2 closed-loop transfer with NASA source/cohort and LOYO/LORO strata separate."

**Impact.** The figure shows 3 of 4 P2 strata and panel c is Former-only, but the caption does not say so. The panel c 60.0% (closed-loop Former) and the text 75.12% (predictive all-44) are different metrics/denominators; a reader may treat them as inconsistent. **Suggested correction.** Add Public LORO to panel a or state the omission; label panel c "Former-Held-Out, closed-loop" and distinguish "closed-loop prior decisions" from "predictive row acceptance" in the caption. This is a figure-reporting fix, not a reason to re-simulate.
