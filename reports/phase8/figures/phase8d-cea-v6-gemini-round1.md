## Detailed Per-Figure Findings

### 1. `fig2-g1.png` — **Verdict: Revise (Blockers)**

* **`[High / Blocker]` `a` (bottom-left) — Twin-axis color collision & spatial scope ambiguity:** `NH4-N` (left axis: `NL deposition (kg N/ha)`, 1980–2026) and `Bare` (right axis: `Surplus (kg N/ha)`, 2015–2026) use the exact same steel-blue color and cross each other around 2015–2023. Combining single-country (`NL`) deposition with `Surplus (kg N/ha)` (whose spatial scope—NL vs 5-region pooled—is unstated) on one twin-axis panel without distinct stroke/hue families invites misreading.
* **`[High / Blocker]` `c` (bottom-left, `Brier skill`) — Missing line-style legend:** Four curves appear (dashed blue/red at ~0.53–0.61 and solid blue/red at ~0.04–0.20), yet only `Sensorless` (red) and `Full-Telemetry` (blue) are legended in `c` top-left. Readers cannot tell what metric or baseline distinguishes dashed from solid curves.
* **`[High / Blocker]` `c` (top-right `CQR width` & bottom-right `Regret`) — Missing bar-pair legend & label-edge collision:** Each row (`Full`, `Sensor`) pairs a colored top bar with an unlegended grey bottom bar. Value transitions (`73.98 → 62.50` / `-15.5%`, etc.) are printed across the inner left bar boundary (colliding with bar edges) and read left-to-right (`grey bottom → colored top`), while the bars are stacked top-to-bottom. Also harmonize `Full-Telemetry / Sensorless` (left legend) vs `Full / Sensor` (right y-ticks).
* **`[High / Blocker]` `b` — Missing legend for marginal KDEs, markers, and background gradient:** Neither the diamond vs dot markers nor the 4-color marginal KDE curves (`blue, red, purple, green`) are legended; `AU` and `CN` both appear in red/rose hues at ~175 kg N/ha, merging visually in both the scatter and top/right KDEs (same `AU` vs `CN` red-hue similarity affects `a` top-left, especially over the rose Test background). The continuous red–white–blue background shading also lacks a colorbar/legend, and the evaluation cohort/policy shown in `b` is unstated.
* **`[Medium]` `a` (middle-right, `Init. N (t/ha)`) — Ambiguous N pool and unit scale:** `Init. N (t/ha)` spans ~5.7–17.5 t/ha (5,700–17,500 kg/ha), whereas every other N axis in the figure uses `kg N/ha`. Specify whether this is *total/organic topsoil N stock (`t N/ha`)* or verify unit scaling if mineral N was intended.
* **`[Medium]` Panel hierarchy (`a`, `c`):** Letter `a` covers four distinct plots (two multi-decadal time series on the left + `Init. N` and `Regimes (%)` + summary table on the right), and `c` covers four distinct plots in a 2×2 grid, preventing unambiguous sub-panel citation (e.g., `a` vs `b` for the right-hand regime block).
* **`[Medium]` `a` (left time series) — Unlabeled background split bands:** Grey, green, blue, and rose vertical spans correspond to `reference / Train / Dev / locked Test` in the x-axis title, but have no direct text headers on the bands.
* **`[Low]` `a` (far-right table), `b`, `c` — Typography & spacing:** The right-hand summary columns (`n  Yield  NUE  S_N`) lack units (`t/ha`, `kg N/ha`), misalign horizontally on single-digit `n = 5` (`NL`), and use raw underscore `S_N` (also in `b`: `S_N 0–40` without units). The regime legend above `a` right floats high above the bars with excess blank space below it; `c` top-left's grey 85–95% tolerance band is unlabeled.
