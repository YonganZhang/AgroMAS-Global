### Substantive findings

1. **The blanket Holm statement is false for non-joint endpoints.** `reports/phase8/PHASE8B_CORE_EXPERIMENTS_G1_G2_G3_REPORT.md:33-34` says "The full 512-test-family Holm-adjusted sign pvalues do not establish superiority." The 512-contrast family contains 21 contrasts with Holm p < .05: 7 `partial_profit`, 7 `Native_N_loss`, and 7 `NUE_gt_090` contrasts (minimum Holm p = 3.814697265625e-06). For the `joint` metric, 0/128 contrasts survive Holm (minimum joint Holm p = .23974609375, the reported Sensorless 3-region PPO example). Thus the statement is correct only for joint compliance, not for the family as a whole. Impact: the report understates statistically significant partial-profit, native-N-loss, and NUE>0.90 differences; it should say "no joint-compliance contrast survives Holm" or report the metric-specific results. Reproduction: count `Holm_sign_p_full_exploratory_family < .05` in `phase8b-core-statistics.json` by `metric`.

2. **Soft-penalty Tier1/2/3 labels are not active-constraint tiers.** `NUE_PCSE-Gym/pcse_gym/agromas_v8/planning.py:56-59` sets `first=np.ones(...)` whenever `regime.soft` and then assigns `tier=1` to every non-OOD soft decision in `choose_tier` (lines 62-63); the soft branch never evaluates the hard `eco_safe`/`NUE_safe` predicates for tier selection. `phase8b-core-statistics.json` `summary.soft.fallback_decision_weighted` therefore reports Tier1 = 0.858974, Tier2 = 0.0, Tier3 = 0.141026, while the region-equal Tier1 average is 2.6333/3 decisions and Tier3 0.3667/3. These are soft-selection/OOD labels, not active-constraint tiers. `phase8b-g2-g3-implementation-contract.md:20` says the soft ablation "reports its active-constraint tiers explicitly"; that is not what the code does. Impact: a reader of the statistics artifact could misread 85.9% Tier1 as hard-constraint compliance. The report's core conclusion (soft 26.73% joint compliance; hard coupling not an established benefit) is unaffected. Reproduction: inspect `planning.py:56-63` and query the soft row in `phase8b-core-statistics.json`.

3. **Recovery provenance is aggregated in a way that masks recovery1's failed completion assertion and the 1454/557 split.** `recovery1/receipt.json` records `expected=2011`, `results=1454`, and `exit_codes=[1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1]`; `phase8b-authorized-recovery1.log` is a traceback ending in `AssertionError` at the script's final `receipt['expected']==receipt['results']` assertion. `recovery2/receipt.json` records `expected=557`, `results=557`, exit codes all 0. `phase8b-combined-campaign-receipt.json` preserves `recovery1_completed=1454`, `recovery2_completed=557`, and `recovery1_attempt_exit_codes` all 1. The core report at line 175 says "The authorized append-only recovery ... added 2011 completed episodes ... Four additional WUR constructor failures ... were repaired ... Those startup records remain preserved", which is true in aggregate but does not state the 1454/557 split or that recovery1 itself failed its completion assertion. Separately, `PHASE8B_NATIVE_HALT_AND_REPAIR_REPORT.md:75` still says "Recovery has NOT run", an obsolete pre-recovery statement not marked historical. Impact: the final 2017 result and hashes are correct, but the recovery narrative can be read as a single clean recovery; the task requires negative findings to remain prominent and unmasked. Reproduction: `cat reports/phase8/recovery1/receipt.json`, `tail reports/phase8/phase8b-authorized-recovery1.log`, `cat reports/phase8/recovery2/receipt.json`, and `grep -n "Recovery has NOT run" reports/phase8/PHASE8B_NATIVE_HALT_AND_REPAIR_REPORT.md`.
