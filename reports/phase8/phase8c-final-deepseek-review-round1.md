## Evidence

### E1 — Blocking: the manuscript's `S_N`/`NUE` definitions contradict the executed endpoint
- **Manuscript text:** `manuscript/phase8/en.tex:40` and `en.md:35`: "Seasonal surplus is fertilizer nitrogen minus harvested grain nitrogen, and nitrogen-use efficiency is harvested grain nitrogen divided by fertilizer input." `zh.tex:40` and `zh.md:35` say the same with "肥料氮" and "籽粒氮除肥料氮".
- **Code:** `NUE_PCSE-Gym/pcse_gym/agromas/season.py:131-133` computes `nonfert = seed_n + 1e4*(RNO3DEPOSTT + RNH4DEPOSTT)`, `inputs = fert + nonfert`, then returns `inputs - grain` as `S_N` (`outcome[2]`) and `grain/inputs` as `NUE` (`outcome[3]`). `seed_n` is 3.5 kg N/ha in all 18,268 cases; deposition is also included. `referee.py:78` and `trajectory_cache.py:75` use `outcome[2]`/`outcome[3]` for `joint`, NUE compliance, subsidy eligibility and profit.
- **Impact:** `reports/phase8/phase8c-native-episode-metrics.csv` gives `S_N - (Executed_N - Grain_N) = 3.5` for every one of the 18,268 cases. Recomputing the stated-definition joint event (`s_text = Executed_N - Grain_N`, `nue_text = Grain_N / Executed_N`) flips 974 cases (5.33%): 440 code-compliant→text-noncompliant and 534 code-noncompliant→text-compliant. Equal-region headline values under the stated definition become: TreeMLP Sensorless soft 30.73% (reported 26.73%), Tier2-Composite 29.06% (25.06%), archived bare 8.00% (4.00%), PPO 26.27% (23.01%); Full soft 21.58% (21.42%), Full bare 13.67% (9.67%). The reported 4.00% and 26.73% are correct for the code's total-input definition, not for the definition printed in the paper.
- **Reproduction:** `python3 - <<'PY'` over `reports/phase8/phase8c-native-episode-metrics.csv`: compute `d = S_N - (Executed_N - Grain_N)`; all 18,268 rows are 3.5. Then compute the text-definition joint and compare with the `joint` column; 974 differ. The same 3.5 offset is visible by comparing any `phase8c-core-statistics.json` summary row's `S_N`, `Executed_N`, `Grain_N` (e.g. `S_N=38.1416`, `Executed_N-Grain_N=34.6416`).
- **Resolution:** The code's total-N-input definition is the standard NUE/surplus definition and is already used consistently by the analysis, contrasts and figures. Correct the Methods/README/report wording to "total nitrogen input (fertilizer plus seed and deposition) minus harvested grain nitrogen" and "grain nitrogen divided by total nitrogen input", then regenerate the md/tex/pdf/report. No rerun is needed and no reported number changes. If the authors instead intend fertilizer-only, the entire campaign would have to be rerun (explicitly forbidden here) and all reported joint/subsidy values would change; that path is not available.

### E2 — Major: sign-test p-value and the equal-region effect are different estimands
- **Text:** `en.tex:55` / `en.md:51` (and `zh.tex:55` / `zh.md:51`) report "3.72 percentage points, with marginal 95% bootstrap interval from −6.18 to 13.64 percentage points, exact sign-test p = 0.5488" for the 52-year equal-region comparison.
- **Contrast record:** the corresponding `all-contrasts.json` GLOBAL_EQUAL5 P1 joint contrast has `n=52`, `n_eff_nonzero=11`, `ties=41`, `mean_difference=0.0371717`, `CI95=[-0.061818, 0.136364]`, `exact_two_sided_sign_p=0.548828125`, `exact_test_weighting="pooled paired farm-year signs/ranks,not region-weighted ranks"`, `bootstrap_weighting="paired years resampled within each region;equal regions"`.
- **Impact:** the 3.72 pp estimate and CI are equal-region, but the 0.5488 p-value is a pooled, tie-dropped farm-year sign test based on only 11 non-tied pairs; it is not the p-value of the equal-region estimand. The text does not disclose `n_eff=11`/41 ties or the pooled weighting. The qualitative conclusion ("not statistical evidence of general superiority") remains conservative, but the reported p/effect pairing is inconsistent and could be over-read.
- **Reproduction:** filter `all-contrasts.json` for `region=="GLOBAL_EQUAL5"`, `benchmark=="P1"`, `cohort=="Former-Held-Out"`, `primary==["TreeMLP","Sensorless","soft"]`, `reference==["SensorlessPPO","Sensorless","tree"]`, `metric=="joint"`; inspect the five fields above.
