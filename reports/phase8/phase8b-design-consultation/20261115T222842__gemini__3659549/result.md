## 3. Avoiding Oracle Future Forecasts & `TRAIN` Historical Weather Analog Marginalization

2. **Fold-Purged Analog Pools**:
   - **During `TRAIN OOF` fold $k$** (validation harvest years $V_k \subset \{1995..2009\}$, 3 years): The regional analog pool is $\mathcal{A}_r^{(-k)} = \{a \in \text{manifest}[r][\text{'Train'}] : a \notin V_k\}$ (`22` complete `Train` years from `1985..2009 \ V_k` in NL/CN/US/FR; `23` from `1984..2009 \ V_k` in AU). Assert `target_harvest_year not in analog_years` on every query so a validation season can **never** draw its own realized future weather!
   - **During `Dev (2010–2014)` Calibration (and `G2/G3`)**: The analog pool is strictly frozen to $\mathcal{A}_r^{\text{TRAIN}} = \text{manifest}[r][\text{'Train'}]$ (`<= 2009`, `25–26` seasons/region). Zero `Dev` (`2010–2014`) or `Held-Out` (`>= 2015`) weather ever enters the analog archive or kernel normalizers.
3. **Automated Invariance Proof**: Assert across `30` synthetic corruptions of target-year weather on dates $> \text{as\_of}$ that analog weights, $K=32$ predicted members, conformal intervals, and selected actions are **bit-for-bit identical (`atol = 0.0`)**.

### 3.2 Causal Analog Construction & Marginalization
At `(region r, as_of, climate c)`:
1. Compute causal prefix descriptor $\phi_{\le t}$ strictly from `history(sow_date, as_of, as_of=as_of)`: `TRAIN`-standardized `[cum_GDD_since_sow, cum_rain_since_sow, rain_last_28d, mean_T_last_14d, mean_rad_last_14d]` concatenated with the frozen Phase 5 4-D 14-day statistical forecast vector.

Compute Gaussian kernel weights $w_a \propto \exp(-\|\tilde{\phi}_{\le t}(\text{target}) - \tilde{\phi}_{\le t}(a)\|_2^2 / (2\tau_{\text{clim}}^2))$ with bandwidth $\tau_{\text{clim}} \in \{1.0, 2.0, \infty\}$ selected strictly on `TRAIN OOF` before touching `Dev`, and deterministically resample $B = 16$ equiprobable analog branches $\{W_{\text{rem}}^{(b)}\}_{b=1}^{16}$ via stratified cumulative-weight quantiles $q_b = (b - 0.5)/16$.

## 4. Pre-Registered Two-Architecture CUDA Lightning Cap

### 4.1 Year-Grouped CV, Prefix-Differential Loss & Fast Factorized Inference
- **Outer Split**: `GroupKFold(n_splits=5)` grouped by `harvest_year` (`1995..2009`: 12 outer-fit years / 3 outer-OOF years per fold). Inside each outer fold, split the 12 fit years into `10 inner-train + 2 inner-val` years for checkpointing/early-stopping so the 3 outer-OOF years stay **100% out-of-sample** for residual and calibrator fitting. Train `3` fixed seeds `(4, 17, 29)` per fold (`AdamW(lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-3)`, `CosineAnnealingLR`, `max_epochs=40`, gradient clip `1.0`), averaging the 3 seeds as the fold predictor $\bar{f}_{\theta^{(-k)}}$ (and refitting `3` seeds on all 15 `TRAIN` years with median inner stopping epoch for locked `Dev`/`Held-Out` evaluation).
- **Within-Prefix Differential Treatment-Effect Loss**: Batch by prefix group $(w, t)$ (sampling multiple legal schedules $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{u}' \in \mathcal{S}(w, t)$ simultaneously) and minimize inverse-cardinality-weighted:
  $$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{Huber}}(\hat{\mathbf{y}}, \mathbf{y}) + \lambda_{\text{diff}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{Huber}}\!\Big((\hat{\mathbf{y}}(\mathbf{u}) - \hat{\mathbf{y}}(\mathbf{u}')),\; (\mathbf{y}(\mathbf{u}) - \mathbf{y}(\mathbf{u}'))\Big) + \lambda_{\text{exec}}\,\text{BCE}\big(\sigma(z_{\text{exec}}),\, \mathbb{1}[\text{no skip}]\big)$$
  ($\lambda_{\text{diff}} = 1.0, \lambda_{\text{exec}} = 0.2$, standardized per target channel inside each fit fold). Differencing within $(w, t)$ cancels noisy inter-annual yield baselines and directly trains marginal schedule slopes $\Delta Y, \Delta N_{\text{grain}}, \Delta N_{\text{exec}}$ that govern `S_N <= S_cap` crossings and within-prefix ranking regret.

### 4.2 Pre-Registered Architecture Candidates (Evaluated on `TRAIN OOF` Before `Dev`)
1. **`Arch-A`: `AgroBiGRUFiLMWorldModel` (~110K params — Mechanistic State-Rollout Bias)**
   - **Past Encoder**: 2-layer GRU (`hidden=64, dropout=0.15`) over $W_{\text{past},\le t}$ fused with causal prefix state $x_{\text{obs},t}$ and $z_{\text{site}} \in \mathbb{R}^4$ (`LayerNorm + SiLU`) to produce latent field state $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$ at `as_of`.
   - **Remaining-Season Rollout**: 1-layer GRU (`hidden=64`) initialized from $h_t$ over $W_{\text{rem}, >t}$ with FiLM conditioning $[\gamma(z_{\text{site}}, c_{\text{regime}}), \beta(z_{\text{site}}, c_{\text{regime}})]$, producing pooled `[terminal_h || attn_pool_h]` $c_{\text{wx}} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$ (encoded **once per `(prefix, analog_branch)`** and broadcast across all candidate schedules $\mathbf{a}_t$ for $<3\text{s}$ full-dataset GPU inference!).
   - **Action-FiLM Head**: 2-block Gated Residual MLP (`dim=128, dropout=0.15`) modulated by FiLM$(\mathbf{a}_t, z_{\text{site}})$ outputting $(z_Y, z_G, z_{\text{exec}})$.
2. **`Arch-B`: `AgroCrossAttnResNetWorldModel` (~160K params — Window Cross-Attention + Zero-Anchored Treatment Decomposition)**

   - **Zero-Anchored Baseline + Treatment Head**:
     $$\hat{\mathbf{y}}(\text{prefix}_t, W_{>t}, \mathbf{u}_{\ge t}) = g_{\text{base}}(x_{\text{obs},t}, c_{\text{wx}}, z_{\text{site}}) + \big[\Psi_{\text{treat}}(x_{\text{obs},t}, c_{\text{wx}}, z_{\text{site}}, \mathbf{a}_t) - \Psi_{\text{treat}}(x_{\text{obs},t}, c_{\text{wx}}, z_{\text{site}}, \mathbf{a}_{t,\mathbf{0}})\big]\mathbb{1}[\Delta U_{\text{nom},\ge t} > 0],$$
     dedicating $g_{\text{base}}$ to soil/weather carry-over at zero future dose and $\Psi_{\text{treat}}$ strictly to nonlinear schedule $\times$ weather response.

## 5. `TRAIN OOF` `K=32` Ensemble, One-Shot `Dev` Conformal, `BSS` & Ranking Regret

### 5.1 Variance-Consistent `K=32` Joint Ensemble (`16 Analogs x 2 Conditional OOF Residuals`)
- **Avoiding Variance Double-Counting**: Total causal prediction error equals **(A) remaining-season weather spread** plus **(B) conditional surrogate error at fixed weather**. Adding marginalized OOF residuals on top of analog branches would double-count weather variance ($2\times \text{Var}(W)$) and fail the `[85%, 95%]` coverage ceiling!
- **Clean Construction**:
  1. On `TRAIN` 5-fold OOF, record **conditional** 3-D joint residuals $\mathbf{e}_i^{\text{cond}} = \mathbf{y}_i - \bar{f}_{\theta^{(-k)}}(\text{prefix}_i, W_{>t,i}^{\text{realized}}, \mathbf{u}_{\ge t,i}) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ (`e_{i,3} = 0` when $\Delta U_{\text{nom}} = 0$), pooled by `(region, stage)` (`>= 1,170` rows/cell even in CN) and sorted by implied $S_N$ residual quantile into a deterministic 32-member representative bank $\{\mathbf{e}^{(1)}, \dots, \mathbf{e}^{(32)}\}$ preserving exact `(Yield, Grain_N, Executed_N_fert)` covariance.
  2. At any causal query, pair the $B = 16$ rainfall-sorted equiprobable `TRAIN` weather analog branches $\{W_{\text{rem}}^{(b)}\}_{b=1}^{16}$ orthogonally with $R = 2$ stratified conditional residual draws ($\alpha_t > 0$ dispersion scale fitted strictly on causal `TRAIN OOF`):
     $$\tilde{\mathbf{y}}^{(k)} = \bar{f}_\theta\big(\text{prefix}_t, W_{\text{rem}}^{(\lceil k/2 \rceil)}, \mathbf{u}_{\ge t}\big) + \alpha_t\,\mathbf{e}^{(\pi(k))}, \quad k = 1, \dots, 32,$$
     and pass `tilde_y` through `project_committed_members` to emit `(N, 32, 6)` physical members. *(Along an individual branch $b$ inside `G2`'s scenario tree, use the 32 conditional residual members directly on $W_{\text{rem}}^{(b)}$.)*

### 5.2 One-Shot `Dev (2010–2014)` Conformal Calibration & `[85%, 95%]` Coverage Gate
- **Normalized CQR Interval**: From the 32 marginalized `S_N` members, let $\hat{\ell}_0 = q_{0.05}(S_N)$, $\hat{u}_0 = q_{0.95}(S_N)$, and $\hat{s} = \max\big(\frac{1}{2}(\hat{u}_0 - \hat{\ell}_0),\, 2.0\big)$. Define nonconformity score $E_i = \max\big((\hat{\ell}_0 - S_{N,i})/\hat{s},\; (S_{N,i} - \hat{u}_0)/\hat{s}\big)$ and calibrated `90%` interval $[\hat{\ell}_0 - \hat{\tau}\hat{s},\; \hat{u}_0 + \hat{\tau}\hat{s}]$.
- **Honest Out-of-Sample `Dev` Verification + Final Freeze**: In-sample conformal fitting on `Dev` would trivially yield `~90%` by resubstitution. Therefore:
  1. Fit $\alpha_t$ and $\hat{\tau}_{\text{TRAIN-OOF}}$ strictly on causal `TRAIN (1995–2009) OOF` (equal `(region, year, stage)` weights) and verify **untouched one-shot empirical `90%` $S_N$ coverage on `Dev (2010–2014)` lies in `[85%, 95%]`** (reporting Leave-One-Dev-Year-Out `LODYO` split-conformal coverage across the 5 `Dev` years alongside).
  2. Once the gate passes, freeze the one-shot finite-sample conformal quantile $\hat{\tau}_{\text{Dev}}$ across the `25` `Dev` site-years for `G2/G3`, exposing calibrated upper quantiles $U_{1-\epsilon}^{\text{conf}}(S_N)$ explicitly in `predict_joint_response` while keeping `CandidateTable` 32-member mass-balance identities exact.

### 5.3 Mandatory `BSS > 0` & Within-Prefix Ranking Regret vs Climatology and Fold-Matched `ExtraTrees`
- **Fold-Matched Full-Schedule `ExtraTreesRegressor` Comparator**: Fit `ExtraTreesRegressor(n_estimators=64, min_samples_leaf=4, random_state=4)` on the **exact same `177,930` reachable rows, same 5 `harvest_year` folds, and same track features** (`Full-Telemetry` and `Sensorless` separately, with full nominal schedule encoding), predicting `(Yield, Grain_N, Executed_N_fert)` and applying the **exact same `TRAIN OOF` residual + `project_committed_members` + probability calibration pipeline** (also logging raw 32-pair `ExtraTrees`) so DL wins only by genuine nonlinear world-model & weather-analog skill.
- **`BSS > 0` Gate**: Because raw `TRAIN` joint compliance is only `6.82%` overall / `8.53%` budget-legal (`0 <= S_N <= 40` is `13.99%`; unilateral `S_N <= 40` is `72.76%`), raw Brier `< 0.15` is vacuous.
